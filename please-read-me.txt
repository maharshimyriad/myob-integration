Stars MYOB AccountRight Connector for WooCommerce
Version 1.0.0

Author:  Aditya Dugar
Website: https://starsdev.com.au/

---

This plugin connects your WooCommerce store to MYOB AccountRight.

When a customer places an order, the plugin automatically:
  - Creates or updates the customer record in MYOB
  - Creates an invoice or sales order in MYOB
  - Syncs inventory levels between MYOB and WooCommerce

Requirements:
  - WordPress 5.0+
  - WooCommerce 2.6+
  - MYOB AccountRight (online company file)
  - PHP 7.1+

Getting Started:
  1. Activate the plugin
  2. Go to WooCommerce → Settings → Integrations → MYOB AccountRight
  3. Click "Validate Access" to connect to your MYOB account
  4. Enter your company file username and click "Connect to Company File"
  5. Select your income, COGS, asset accounts and tax codes
  6. Save settings
